Today, a customer's technical question routes through a senior expert and waits two or three days for a reply. Sense is an agentic AI workspace that brings that down to minutes. Agents handle routing, drafting, and source-checking inside a workspace shaped to your team's flows. Your team asks in their native language; replies come as drafts with cited sources from your own documents, in whichever format the moment calls for (technical report, proposal, email reply, and others). Send after a quick review. Designed around your domain's taxonomy, in the words your team actually uses: each reply quietly trains the workspace, so the next colleague gets the answer instantly.

Your data stays on your server, no outbound data. Sense reads from the platforms you already use, no disruption to current operations. Single self-contained install, free to evaluate, with a tailored advisor file backing the workflows that matter. Ready to build into your stack and tune to your team's flows; data architecture and indexing can be designed alongside when needed.

Open to teams ready to explore Sense at their own rhythm. You start with a shared sandbox so your team has time to sit around Sense together, explore at their own pace, and imagine what their workflow could look like inside it. A short feature request form tells us what works for you, and from there we move directly into customised build and deployment alignment, in steps. No separate pilot scope, no upfront commitment. Where I've operated

Two tracks, one practice.

A scientist who codes and speaks the domain vocabulary.
