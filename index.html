Operational Memory

Onboard light. Know-how outlasts churn. Memory holds.

Today a technical question (customer inquiry, internal R&D assessment, feasibility check) routes through a senior expert and waits two or three days for a reply. Sense 2026 turns that wait into minutes: one inquiry stays in one workspace across days, hand-offs, and reviewers. It runs on an ontology your AI agents act on, shaped to your sub-domain's taxonomy and your team's vocabulary. An inquiry past your risk threshold auto-routes for senior review; a missing field trips your block list before a draft leaves. Where general AI hands you a guess, every Sense answer carries its source and reliability tier. Year by year, your intelligence compounds.

What compounds is what you tune. Where many platforms keep configuration on the vendor side, all three layers stay with your team: catalogue, pricing, and approval rules tuneable in-house; reliability cut-offs and methodology toggles dialled inquiry by inquiry; deeper structure (classifier, risk patterns, scoring weights) architected with us via your advisor file. Pricing is flat per organisation, not per seat. Evaluation is free.

Open to teams ready to explore Sense at their own pace. You don't need to fit the prototype. The shared sandbox is a brainstorming canvas, not the final shape of your platform: your team sits around Sense, explores, and tells us what clicks (or doesn't). We build tailored solutions through back-and-forth, then deploy in steps. Bring your hardest workflow when the time feels right, and reach out→ to tell us where to start.

A scientist who codes and speaks the domain vocabulary. Operational vocabulary came from holding each role in the chain (bench scientist, bioinformatician, ops executive, commercial lead), forming the translation layer that fits each context, not one-size-fits-all, now in senior advisory (Partnership) and agentic AI tooling (Sense 2026).
