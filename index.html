Sense 2026

Query to quote in a minute.

Today, a customer's technical question routes through a senior expert and waits two or three days for a reply. Sense brings that down to minutes. Your team asks in plain English, gets a draft with cited sources pulled from your own documents and past answers, and sends after a quick review. Built around your domain's taxonomy, in your team's language: when a question is missing context, Sense asks back or shows where to confirm it, instead of returning a flood of related-but-irrelevant results. Each reply quietly trains the system, so the next colleague who asks something similar gets the answer instantly.

Your data stays on your server. The software runs as a single self-contained install, free to evaluate, with a tailored advisor file backing the workflows that matter to your business. The prototype backbone is ready, and I'm recruiting design partners for a 4-week build phase plus a 30-day trial program. Built for teams writing the same answer to different customers every other week, while those customers wait days for what already exists somewhere in the company. If that sounds like yours, bring one real workflow and test the prototype against your hardest week.
