How I work

A simple path from first call to deliverable.

Most engagements start with a free 30-minute discovery call. If there's a fit, I scope a written proposal with deliverables, timeline, and fees before any work begins.

01
Discovery call

Free 30-minute conversation to understand the situation, the constraint, and whether I'm the right person.

02
Scoped proposal

Written scope, deliverables, timeline, and fees. Nothing starts until you've agreed in writing.

03
Delivery

Async-friendly working rhythm with structured checkpoints. Deliverables you can act on, not slide decks that sit on a drive.

Two-week sprint
A bounded deliverable: market scan, technical audit, partnership shortlist.
Fractional advisory
Ongoing engagement at a defined cadence (usually weekly or bi-weekly) for leadership teams.
One-time deep dive
Scientific due diligence, ISO/LIMS audit, or capability assessment for a specific decision.
